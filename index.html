Dominic Behrens

Housing and urban economist. Doing absolutely everything I can to fix the housing crisis.

I am a Senior Economist at The Centre for International Economics. I work on housing, land use and transport, using quantitative analysis and modelling to answer practical policy questions. I also co-founded Sydney YIMBY.
